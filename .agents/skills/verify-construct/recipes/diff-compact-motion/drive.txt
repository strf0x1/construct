# Motion on (drive.env). Turn one: an edit's diff gets a white highlighter
# pass left to right once it has trickled in. Turn two: a dozen big reads fill
# the 16k window and construct compacts: a lavender wash over the transcript
# above the `◇ compacted history` notice clears from the top down into it, and
# the notice resolves out of block glyphs. Each lasts about a second, so each
# is snapped several times in a row.
